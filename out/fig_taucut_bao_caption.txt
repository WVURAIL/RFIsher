Figure: what the discarded k_par window is worth.

BAO detection significance A/sigma(A) versus the delay cut tau_cut for three configurations. CHIME as published in the 2025 auto-correlation detection: 2,200 deg^2, 608.2-707.8 MHz in three dz ~ 0.11 bins, 385 h of effective integration from 94 nights, 0.5 mJy/beam per frequency channel, Tsys_tot = 55 K. The seven-year archive on the full Overview sky: 31,000 deg^2, 400-800 MHz in 15 bins, seven calendar years at the 2019 cosmology-quality duty cycle = 9,327 h = 1.06 on-sky years. The same archive on the sky the present pipeline accepts, |y| < 0.4 (declinations 25.7-72.9 deg, 10,756 deg^2) at the same per-voxel depth, i.e. t_tot scaled with the area: a declination cut loses volume without deepening what it keeps. The published analysis filters at tau_cut = 200 ns and discards everything below the 280 ns mask, so it begins at k_par = 0.35 h/Mpc; the top axis gives k_par,min at z = 1.16 for each tau_cut, including that 1.4x transition factor. Contamination residuals are zero: this prices the modes the cut removes, not the leakage left in the modes it keeps.

Thresholds, read off the grid as the bracket [last delay at or above, first delay below], sigma_NL = 7 Mpc:
  chime2025            5 sigma:  never reached   3 sigma:       50-75 ns   no cut: 5.25
  archive7yr           5 sigma:     125-140 ns   3 sigma:     140-150 ns   no cut: 50.19
  archive7yr_accepted  5 sigma:     110-125 ns   3 sigma:     125-140 ns   no cut: 29.56

Noise-model calibration. The total power-spectrum S/N over 0.4 < k < 1.5 h/Mpc with tau_cut = 200 ns is sqrt(F_pk,pk) = sqrt(sum over modes of (P_sig / sigma_P)^2), a single-amplitude total S/N and not the wiggle amplitude. At the fiducial sigma_NL = 7 Mpc it gives 2.2 against the published 12.4 (0.18x); with the small-scale damping removed it gives 88 (7.1x); sigma_NL = 4.9 Mpc reproduces 12.4. The statistic is sensitive to sigma_NL because RadioFisher applies exp(-mu^2 k^2 sigma_NL^2) to the whole 21 cm power and the delay cut leaves only the mu ~ 1 modes that term acts on; it is a measurement of small-scale damping, not of the noise. The noise normalisation is checked uncut: the same band with no delay cut gives 10.4 (0.84x), within 1.5x, so t_tot is not rescaled (x5.6 would be needed under the cut). The BAO sweep runs at the fiducial sigma_NL; at the matched value the thresholds move by
  chime2025            5 sigma:  never reached ->  never reached   3 sigma:       50-75 ns ->       50-75 ns
  archive7yr           5 sigma:     125-140 ns ->     140-150 ns   3 sigma:     140-150 ns ->     160-175 ns
  archive7yr_accepted  5 sigma:     110-125 ns ->     110-125 ns   3 sigma:     125-140 ns ->     140-150 ns
RadioFisher's k_NL cutoff (0.14 Mpc^-1 at z = 0) lands at 0.35 h/Mpc at z = 1.16 and deletes the entire detection band; it is lifted for the calibration step only and restored for the sweep.
